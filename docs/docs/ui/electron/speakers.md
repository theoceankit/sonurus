# Speakers

The Speakers window of the UI (`ui/src/screens/Speakers/`): every speaker the app knows, their name, colour, photo and voice, the recordings they talk in, and Delete. Its routes are in [API endpoints](../../api/endpoints.md).

## Opening

The Speakers button at the right of the titlebar opens `SpeakersDialog`, which `EditorScreen` holds. Each opening starts on the list, on All speakers. It is a large modal laid out as Choose a speaker: the categories in a column at its side and the list or a card next to them, in one height (`--size-speakers-pane`) whatever is shown. The window closes by its close button or Escape. A card's name and handle are saved only with its Save; the colour and the photo are kept at once from the Avatar window; the list has nothing to save.

## Categories and the list

- **Categories.** All speakers, Recognized (speakers with a name) and Unnamed, each with its count. Unnamed has its own icon, `speaker-unknown`: a speaker with a question mark. Recently added is marked with `markNotImplemented()` until the API knows when a speaker was added.
- **Search.** Matches any part of the name or of the handle, whatever the case and the spaces around it; a leading `@` is left out, so `@lena` finds `@lena.kovacs`. A speaker without a name never matches a search (`filterSpeakers()` in `speakers.ts`).
- **Rows.** `Candidate` with the avatar, the name or Unnamed speaker in grey with the handle under it, the number of recordings and the time the speaker talks (`speakerUsage()`). Speakers whose names differ only by case or spaces get a same name badge (`duplicateNameIds()`): names are labels, not identities, so two people may share one; their handles differ.
- **Order.** As `GET /speakers` gives it: named speakers by name, then those without a name, most recently heard first. Speakers that tie, such as two with one name, keep one order, the one heard last first.
- **States.** Loading, a failure with Retry, no speakers at all (they appear after a transcription), no match for the search, and an empty category.
- **New speaker** at the left of the footer is marked with `markNotImplemented()`: a speaker is still made by assigning segments to them in the Editor.

## A speaker's card

A click on a row opens `SpeakerCard` in place of the list; the categories stay, and Back to speakers returns to the list. The card goes from who the speaker is to where they talk: avatar, name and handle, then usage, the voice sample and Appears in. More, at the top right, holds Merge and Delete. A speaker that goes while their card is open (deleted, or all data reset) takes the card back to the list.

- **Name.** Edited in place; typing and leaving the field save nothing. Enter is Save, and Escape in the field takes the card's edits back and keeps the window open. A blank name is not saved, so Save stays off with one. A name another speaker has is allowed and said under the field. A refusal of the backend is shown at the field. A saved name shows everywhere: in the list, the sidebar, the transcript and the inspector.
- **Handle.** The speaker's handle, with `@` in front of the field ([Speaker handles](../../api/endpoints.md#speaker-handles)). Until the field is typed in, a new name in the card shows the handle it brings (`GET /speakers/handle-suggestion`, the speaker left out), numbered if taken, unless the handle was set by hand and stays. What is typed turns lowercase and loses a leading `@`, and Save sends it as a handle set by hand. Emptied, the field shows the handle made from the name as its placeholder and says it comes back with Save. A handle that is not one, or that another speaker has, is said under the field (`handleProblem()` in `handles.ts`) and keeps Save off; a refusal of the backend shows there too. A speaker without a name has none: the field is read-only and says the handle is made from the name. Enter is Save.
- **Merge into another speaker** in the More menu is marked with `markNotImplemented()`, for merging still to come.
- **Colour.** Chosen in the Avatar window (below) among the five speaker colours, and kept at once (`PATCH /speakers/{id}` with `color_index` alone). It is not part of the card's draft, so Save and Cancel stay as they were; the window shows the chosen colour at once, the card once the speakers are read again. A speaker without a name is grey, and the card says a colour and a photo come with a name. It does not promise that a name makes the speaker recognized: recognition comes from the voice, which only assigning segments in the Editor teaches.
- **Avatar window.** The avatar of a speaker with a name is a button (Change avatar), and takes a picture dropped on it too. It opens `AvatarModal`: the avatar at its largest, where a picture can be dropped, the formats it takes (JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 20 MB), Upload photo or Change photo, Remove photo when there is one, and the colour. A picture, chosen or dropped, is cropped to the circle in place of the avatar, in the same window, and its Save photo uploads it at once (`PUT /speakers/{id}/photo`, `useSetSpeakerPhoto()`), apart from the card's Save, which it neither needs nor turns on; Remove photo takes it away (`DELETE /speakers/{id}/photo`). The window stays open for more, and Done closes it; a refusal shows in it. The photo then shows in every avatar of the speaker: the list, the card, the transcript, the inspector, the speaker menus, Choose a speaker and the stacks of the sidebar. A speaker without a name has none, and their avatar opens nothing. See [Speaker photos](../../api/endpoints.md#speaker-photos) and, for the controls, [Foundations](foundations.md#speaker-photos).
- **Usage.** The recordings, the time the speaker talks and when they were last heard.
- **Voice sample.** The segment `GET /speakers/{id}/sample` picks, at most fifteen seconds, in a `VoiceClip`, the same as the clip of the inspector's Review card: the player, and its text folded under Show text. While the sample loads, Play is off and there is no text yet. When no recording of the speaker has its audio any more, the card says so instead, and a speaker in no recording says Not in any recording yet.
- **Appears in.** The recordings of `GET /speakers/{id}/transcripts`, newest first, as `AppearanceRow`: the title and the date. A click closes the window and opens the recording in the Editor. Play stands over the date on hover and focus and plays a sample of the speaker from that recording. A recording whose file is gone (`has_audio` false) has Play off from the start, with Audio unavailable as its tooltip.
- **Save and Cancel** stand at the right of the footer while a card is open, both off until something is edited. Save sends the name and handle in one `PATCH /speakers/{id}` (`draftChanges()` in `speakers.ts`) and is done once the speakers are read again; Cancel takes the edits back. The edits live in `SpeakersDialog` (`SpeakerDraft`), so they go with the card: Back, a category, closing the window or opening a recording from Appears in first ask Discard changes? (`DiscardChangesDialog`), with Keep editing and Discard. A card without edits leaves at once.
- **Delete speaker** in the More menu, right under Merge, asks first in a danger dialog (`DeleteSpeakerDialog`, focus on Cancel) with the text of `deleteSpeakerPrompt()`: how many segments in how many recordings become Unassigned, and that the voice profile goes. `DELETE /speakers/{id}` then removes the name, colour, photo and voice; the recordings and the speaker list are asked for again, and the next speaker of the list opens, or the one before, or the list. While the queue transcribes or a recording runs it does not ask but shows the reason in a toast (`deleteBlockReason()` on the queue snapshot), as a menu item cannot show a tooltip; the backend refuses then too.

## Samples

`useSamplePlayback()` (`ui/src/media/sample-playback.ts`) plays one sample at a time on an audio element of its own, so the Editor's player keeps its place and pauses while a sample plays, as a clip of the inspector does. A sample may come from any recording: the player loads the recording by its id (`getRecordingAudioUrl()`, `GET /transcripts/{id}/audio`) and plays once it is ready. A second Play pauses it; closing the window or another card stops it.
